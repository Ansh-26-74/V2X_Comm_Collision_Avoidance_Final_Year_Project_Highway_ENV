Time-To-Collision (TTC) Kinematics

Deterministic physical safety foundation

Physical Formulation
Closing Speed V_closing = V_ambulance - V_lead
Time-To-Collision TTC = (Bumper Gap) / V_closing
TTC > 4.0 s SAFE Nominal following speed; priority emergency cruising permitted.
2.0 s – 4.0 s WARNING Closing rate elevated; prepare lateral passing corridor.
TTC < 2.0 s CRITICAL Immediate collision threat; mandatory evasive lane change or braking.
🛡️
Safety Governance Hierarchy: TTC is authoritative for immediate physical safety. AI trajectory prediction is strictly advisory and cannot override physical TTC limits.

Experimental Methodology

  • Paired Trials: 10 controlled runs across varying signal offsets.
  • Total Executions: 20 headless deterministic simulation runs.
  • Physics & Kinematics: Strictly identical 60 Hz timesteps.
  • Traffic Conditions: Identical PRNG seeds and civilian spawns.
  • Baseline Mode: Normal cycle; emergency preemption disabled.
  • V2I Mode: DSRC request → conflict check → preemption.
  • Timeouts / Failures: 0 timeouts, 0 collisions, 0 safety violations.
💡

What the Measurements Show

The measured reduction in total travel time (mean -2.70 s / 23.8%) is primarily associated with reduced signal waiting time (mean -2.70 s / 54.3%).

Intersection clearance time remained strictly unchanged (1.73 s vs 1.73 s), and full stop counts were identical (0.80 vs 0.80).

This indicates that, under these controlled simulation conditions, the measured benefit emerges from eliminating signal-induced delay rather than altering physical crossing velocity.
